# Privacy Policy for ATHLETX

**Effective Date:** September 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
ATHLETX is designed to respect your privacy. The app stores your workout data, settings, and profile information locally on your device. We collect sensitive personal data such as your age, weight, height, and injuries strictly for personalizing your workout plans and tracking progress.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- **Local Data:** Workout history, custom plans, preferences, and physical characteristics (age, weight, height, injuries) are stored locally on your device to provide the core functionality of the app. This data never leaves your device.
- **Advertising:** We use Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID and other information to serve personalized ads.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All personal and sensitive data remains securely on your device. We encourage you to use your device's built-in security features (such as a passcode or biometrics) to protect your local data.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use Google AdMob for advertising. Google may use the advertising ID from the device on which the ad is serving to generate interests and demographics. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services [here](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). If applicable based on your region (e.g., EU or UK), you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

## 5. Your Rights
You have full control over your local data. You can delete your workouts, custom plans, or reset the app entirely from the Settings menu, which will clear all local data. You may also reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads through your device settings.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the repository's issue tracker or support channels.
