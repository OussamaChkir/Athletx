# Privacy Policy for ATHLETX

**Effective Date:** September 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
ATHLETX is designed to respect your privacy. The app primarily stores your workout data, settings, and profile information locally on your device.

If you choose to use the "Connected Accounts" (Google) feature, we may receive basic profile information (such as your email address and name) provided by Google to facilitate authentication and personalization.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- **Local Data:** Workout history, custom plans, and preferences are stored locally on your device to provide the core functionality of the app.
- **Account Data:** If connected to Google, your email is used solely for authentication and account continuity. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Most of your data remains on your device. We encourage you to use your device's built-in security features (such as a passcode or biometrics) to protect your local data.

## 4. Your Rights
You have full control over your local data. You can delete your workouts, custom plans, or reset the app entirely from the Settings menu, which will clear all local data.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the repository's issue tracker or support channels.
